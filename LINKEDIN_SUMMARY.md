# LinkedIn case-study summary

I lead product direction for Acorda, an Android app in active beta development focused on daily planning, local photo/audio evidence, and guided breathing. My work includes product requirements, UX/UI direction, interaction design, feature prioritization, acceptance criteria, and validation planning.

I coordinate implementation with AI-assisted software development tools, then review the resulting behavior and test evidence against the product requirements. The project includes Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Room persistence, and Android's on-device SpeechRecognizer. A key design focus is keeping personal evidence local and making Free/Pro access changes predictable without deleting saved records.

The product is pre-release. Google Play Billing, purchase verification, and commercial launch are not complete. This summary describes product ownership and delivery coordination; it does not claim that I manually wrote every line of source code.
